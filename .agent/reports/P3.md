## Predictions recorded before implementation

The existing verifier will accept a changed `sampled_hierarchy.upper_mass`, a
changed `effective_mass.best_upper_mass`, and omission of an entire configured
model/radius group if the remaining comparison rows, certificate indices and
counters are made internally consistent. These are failures of saved-output
binding and completeness, not counterexamples to the individual exact rational
weight certificates. The regression tests will also require the configured
noise inventory, the full hierarchy-order and effective-pair inventories, their
status/reason fields and the declared weight width to remain bound to inputs.

Acceptance requires rejection of these mutations, genuine generated output
passing, and a read-only full Arb reintegration passing. Tests alone do not
establish any claim about full QED or physical truncation error.

## Reproduction and cause

Before the fix, the two assigned test files produced **17 failed, 26 passed**.
In particular the `-999` sampled/effective headline mutations, false weight
width, and internally consistent omission of a configured radius group still
returned `PASS`. The verifier checked arithmetic for present records, but the
dataset inventory was derived from present groups and several aggregate fields
and per-order/per-pair inventories were not bound.

## Change

- Require the full configured model x reference radius x noise Cartesian
  inventory, unique combinations and configuration-bound dataset identifiers.
- Bind available prefixes, every hierarchy order, the sampled highest-order
  summary, the best available hierarchy bound, every effective-mass pair and
  its minimum, and the weight-width summary.
- Recheck each exact rational vector's numerator, denominator, valid ratio,
  status and reason. A genuine numerical pencil refusal remains `NO_BOUND` and
  is rechecked; it cannot replace a successful solve. A floating proposed
  eigenvector is allowed to vary across LAPACK versions and is never the proof.
- Require the complete independent-check index inventory and true summary
  success flags. Preserve configuration hashes, all existing cardinality and
  certificate-bijection checks, read-only operation and the `python -O` guard.
