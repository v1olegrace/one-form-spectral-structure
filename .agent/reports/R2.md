## Independence

The two routes share only the Euler–Heisenberg Lagrangian.

- **Route A** is an exact rational computation: the vertex, −g polarization
  sums, and the Dalitz simplex integral.
- **Route B** uses GP's configuration-space sunset computation. Portolés and
  Ruiz-Femenía reach the same QCD coefficient through a different Fourier
  route.
