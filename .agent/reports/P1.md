# P1 — RPA discrete-support theorem

Date: 2026-10-23. Scope: the controlled positive-density Dyson model.

## Prediction recorded before changes

The generic Proposition 4 will fail for a density that vanishes at its upper
endpoint. The exact witness `rho(s)=(10-s) 1_[4,10]`, `g2=1/100` has positive
`Z3` and a strictly positive upper-edge limit, hence no upper atom.
The missing condition is an endpoint integral large enough to cross zero;
divergence is sufficient and holds for the hard-cutoff Dirac density.

Internal gaps can contain additional positive atoms in every Z3 regime. The
exact critical witness `rho=1_[4,5]+1_[9,10]`, `g2=1/log(25/18)` has an atom
at `s=7`, weight `3/7`. Generic no-atom and completeness statements must be
narrowed; the continuous positive Dirac model remains in its previous domain.

Predicted outcome: counterexamples retract the overgeneral statements;
corrected conditional proofs retain the concrete Dirac result. Automated
checks will verify exact identities and independently evaluate the witnesses;
these checks are not interval certification.
