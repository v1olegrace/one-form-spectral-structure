## Cause of the numerical error

`mp.quad` stops once its error estimate is below 2^(4−prec) in absolute
terms. The profiles at r ≳ 50 are far below that, so they were integrated at
the coarsest level. The result was stable under a change of `dps` and wrong.

The fix is `_quad_relative`. It divides the integrand by an a priori bound
before integrating:

- for the Gaussian, K_2(r) + ρ_R(r)/4;
- for an exterior factor, e^{−2r}F(2), valid because F'/F ≤ a.

It also refuses a result whose error estimate is not small relative to the
value. A test checks that refusal on a kinked integrand of size 1e-200, where
plain `mp.quad` silently returns 0.2762 for 5/18.

Evidence: `.agent/evidence/R0-quadrature.json` (old and new values at 30–90
digits, exact moment ratios and roots).
