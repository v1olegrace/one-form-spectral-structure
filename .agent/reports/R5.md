## Predictions and acceptance criteria, recorded before computing

Method: the full one-loop amplitude γ*(q) → γγγ for a Dirac loop of mass m,
from a Feynman-parameter representation summed over the six orderings. It is
used in two ways:

- exact rational coefficients of its low-energy expansion (orders λ⁴, λ⁶ and,
  if feasible, λ⁸ in the external momenta), for the s⁴, s⁵ and s⁶ terms of ρ;
- a double-precision evaluation of the full amplitude, only above a
  separation scale s_c, because the six orderings cancel to O(λ⁴) at small s.

**Acceptance — the computation is rejected if any of these fails.**

1. **Ward identities.** The amplitude vanishes when any one polarization is
   replaced by its momentum, including the virtual leg, contracted with q.
2. **Bose symmetry** in the three photons.
3. **EH limit.** The λ⁴ term reproduces route A exactly:
   Σ|Q|² = s⁴[(139/9)e₂² − (148/9)e₃], ∫S = 17/45.
4. **Constants cancel.** The momentum-independent constants from the l⁴
   terms cancel across the orderings. Checked numerically, not assumed.
5. **Independent on-shell check.** The on-shell γγ→γγ amplitude from the same
   code is compared with a published value beyond the leading ω⁶ term, if
   one can be read. On-shell data cannot test the q²-dependent structures;
   for those, only criteria 1 and 3 apply.

**Predictions.**

- The s⁵ coefficient b, in ρ = K s⁴[1 + b s/(4m²) + …], has natural size:
  |b| between 0.2 and 5. Its sign is not predicted.
- The full density divided by K s⁴ is smooth on 0 < s < 4m² and stays
  within a factor 3 of 1 up to s = 3m².
- With the full O(α⁴) density, the radii shift by less than 0.3/m:
  r_Γ = 20.70 ± 0.3 and r_× = 22.60 ± 0.3.
- **Pair channel.** Replacing the one-loop pair density by the O(α²)
  Källén–Sabry density, without adding a separate Sommerfeld factor, shifts
  the radii by less than 0.1/m.

**If the full amplitude does not validate:** deliver the exact s⁵ (and s⁶)
coefficients with their successive-term pattern, and keep the tail labelled
unbounded.
