**Use.**

- Theorem 2.2(iii) gives closure of the Stieltjes class under pointwise limits.
  Its proof is the compactification argument used for Proposition 3b of the
  RPA note.
- The remark on p. 18 is exactly the critical-case phenomenon: subcritical
  propagators have no constant, but their limit has the constant 1/μ.

Both are cited as confirmation. The note's proofs do not depend on them, and
the direct proofs are kept.
