### C4. Interval arithmetic

`mpmath.iv` is used for the interval-certified tests. Where a quantity is
computed with `mp.quad` (which carries **no** rigorous error bound), the result
is labelled `CHECKED`, never `CERTIFIED`. Only bounds that are monotone
functionals of interval-valued *samples* (Theorem G / eq. (24)–(25)) are
labelled `CERTIFIED`, because there the error propagation is a proved
deterministic inequality rather than a quadrature estimate.
